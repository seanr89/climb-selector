# Quickstart for ClimbRoute AI

This document provides a quick overview of the ClimbRoute AI feature and how to test it.

## Feature Summary

ClimbRoute AI is a mobile and web application that helps indoor climbers visualize routes on a climbing wall. Users can take a photo of the wall, and the app will identify the climbing holds and their colors. The user can then select a color to highlight the corresponding route, making it easier to plan their climb.

## Testing the Feature

To test the ClimbRoute AI feature, follow these steps:

1.  **Launch the application.**
2.  **Capture a photo of a climbing wall.** Ensure the wall has colored climbing holds.
3.  **Verify the color palette.** The app should display a palette of colors detected from the holds in the image.
4.  **Select a color.** Tap on a color in the palette.
5.  **Verify the route highlighting.** The holds of the selected color should be highlighted (using selective color), and all other holds should be grayscaled.
6.  **Reset the filter.** Find and tap the reset button.
7.  **Verify the image is reset.** The image should return to its original, unfiltered state.
